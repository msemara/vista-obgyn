Endometriosis care

Endometriosis care in Montclair, NJ

Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus, causing pain, inflammation, and often fertility issues. At Vista Women's Health, we offer personalized evaluation and treatment options to help manage symptoms and improve your quality of life.

Whether you're newly diagnosed or have been dealing with endometriosis for years, we provide support, answers, and solutions tailored to your needs.

Symptoms

Common symptoms of endometriosis.

Endometriosis can affect each person differently. You may experience:

  • Pelvic pain before or during your period
  • Pain during or after intimacy
  • Heavy or irregular periods
  • Pain with bowel movements or urination
  • Fatigue or digestive discomfort
  • Difficulty getting pregnant

About the condition

Understand what endometriosis is.

  • What it isEndometriosis is a condition in which cells similar to those of the lining of your uterus abnormally grow elsewhere in your body. It affects approximately 200 million women worldwide and can cause pain, infertility, and problems with intimacy.
  • DiagnosisThe pattern of your symptoms and a physical exam can sometimes give Dr. Shilad enough information to diagnose endometriosis, but laparoscopic surgery and biopsy is the only way to confirm the diagnosis. Imaging tests are inconclusive, and no blood test can detect the condition.
  • TreatmentPain and irregular bleeding are sometimes manageable with medications like nonsteroidal anti-inflammatories (NSAIDs) and ones that control your hormones like the pill, progestins, and androgens. Dr. Shilad may recommend surgery when your symptoms are severe or do not respond well to noninvasive therapies. About minimally invasive surgery
