What we treat

We offer comprehensive obstetrics & gynecology care in Montclair.

Our OB/GYN team includes highly skilled, board-certified physicians with expertise in a wide range of specialties, supported by a dedicated group of nurses and midwives. Many of our nurses hold certifications in women's health, and our midwives provide both in-office care and labor support in the hospital. Together, we offer compassionate, expert care in the following areas:

  • Well woman examinations
  • Routine gynecological care
  • Menstrual problems
  • Urinary incontinence
  • Abnormal Pap smears
  • Infections
  • Uterine disorders
  • Pelvic pain
  • Menopause management
  • Low-risk and minimally invasive procedures
  • Da Vinci Robot System-assisted surgeries for faster recovery and improved outcomes

By the numbers

Gynecological health statistics in the U.S.

  • Annual gynecological visits

    65–70%

    According to a report by the CDC, around 65–70% of women between the ages of 18 and 44 visit a healthcare provider for gynecological care at least once a year.

  • Contraceptive use

    99%

    The Guttmacher Institute reports that 99% of sexually active women who do not wish to become pregnant use contraception at some point in their life.

  • Maternal health

    3.6 million

    The CDC also notes that in 2020, about 3.6 million babies were born in the U.S., highlighting the importance of obstetric and gynecological care for pregnant women and their babies.

About us

About Vista Women's Health PA.

At Vista Women's Health, we understand the importance of making our patients feel comfortable as soon as they walk in our door. Dr. Shilad, Stephanie Diaz, CNM (certified nurse midwife) and our entire staff are dedicated to providing you with the very best care, respect, and education regarding your gynecological health. Professionalism and expertise are provided at every level. We believe in taking a personalized and collaborative approach to your gynecological health. Our providers fully explain conditions, procedures, and discuss your health concerns in terms that are easy to understand so that you can make educated decisions regarding your own health and well-being.

In addition to well woman examinations and routine care, our providers regularly address other gynecological issues with our patients, including menstrual problems, urinary incontinence, abnormal Pap smears, infections, uterine disorders, pelvic pain, menopause, and much more.

Dr. Shilad is a highly regarded surgeon who has mastered the latest technologies in low-risk and minimally invasive procedures that require only small incisions and facilitate shorter recovery times so you can get back to your life. Dr. Shilad is certified in the da Vinci Robot System which is designed to provide faster recovery and improved patient outcomes compared to traditional procedures.
