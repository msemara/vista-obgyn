Fibroid care

Uterine fibroid care in Montclair, NJ

Fibroids are non-cancerous growths in or around the uterus that can cause pain, heavy periods, and other symptoms depending on their size and location.

At Vista Women's Health, we help you understand how fibroids may be affecting your health and offer treatment options suited to your needs and goals, whether you're looking to manage symptoms or planning for pregnancy.

Symptoms

Common symptoms of fibroids.

  • Heavy or prolonged menstrual bleeding
  • Pelvic pressure or fullness
  • Frequent urination
  • Pain during intimacy
  • Lower back or leg pain
  • Difficulty getting pregnant or maintaining pregnancy

About the condition

Understand fibroids: diagnosis and treatments.

  • How common they areUterine fibroids are extremely common among women of childbearing years, though some cases remain undiagnosed. The National Institutes of Health estimates about half of all women have fibroids by age 50.

Why Vista Women's Health

Why choose Vista Women's Health for fibroid treatment?

At Vista Women's Health, we offer expert care in diagnosing and treating uterine fibroids. Our team provides tailored treatment plans that range from non-invasive options to surgical solutions, ensuring we address your specific needs. If Dr. Shilad suspects fibroids are the cause of your pelvic pain he may perform imaging such as a pelvic ultrasound.
