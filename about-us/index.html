Your physician

Meet Dr. Shilad

Aiman K. Shilad, MD · Obstetrician-gynecologist and minimally invasive gynecologic surgeon

Dr. Aiman Shilad is a highly skilled and compassionate medical professional dedicated to providing exceptional care in the field of obstetrics and gynecology located in Montclair, New Jersey. With years of experience and a strong commitment to women's health, Dr. Shilad has earned a reputation for delivering comprehensive and personalized care to his patients. Dr. Shilad is certified in the da Vinci Robot System which is designed to provide faster recovery and improved patient outcomes compared to traditional procedures.

Dr. Shilad completed his medical degree at St. George's University School of Medicine. After earning his medical degree, he completed his residency in obstetrics and gynecology at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai in New York City. He is currently completing his MBA at Yale University. Dr. Shilad is a member of the American Congress of Obstetricians and Gynecologists and continues to be recognized as a New Jersey Top Doctor every year.

Known for his warm demeanor and empathetic approach, Dr. Shilad creates a comfortable and supportive environment for his patients. He believes in forming strong relationships with his patients, actively listening to their concerns, and involving them in the decision-making process. His dedication to patient-centered care ensures that each individual receives personalized treatment plans tailored to their unique needs and circumstances.

Beyond his clinical practice, Dr. Shilad is actively involved in medical research and education. He remains up-to-date with the latest advancements in his field and is committed to providing evidence-based care. Driven by his passion for sharing knowledge, he has presented at numerous conferences and published articles in reputable medical journals, contributing to the advancement of women's health care.

Dr. Shilad's patients consistently commend him for his exceptional clinical skills, compassionate bedside manner, and genuine dedication to their well-being. His commitment to excellence and his unwavering advocacy for women's health make him a trusted and respected healthcare provider.

Dr. Aiman Shilad is honored to serve his patients and looks forward to making a positive impact on the lives of women, helping them achieve optimal health and well-being.

Your midwife

Meet our midwife

Stephanie Diaz, CNM · Certified nurse midwife

Stephanie Diaz is a highly skilled and compassionate midwife dedicated to providing exceptional care and support to expectant mothers and their families. With years of experience and a deep commitment to the well-being of mothers and babies, Stephanie Diaz is here to ensure that your birthing experience is nothing short of extraordinary. In addition, Stephanie specializes in the care of women through all life stages, from adolescence through menopause, by providing well woman care which includes but is not limited to annual exams, which include a Pap smear, breast exam, and STD screening, prevention, and treatment (if necessary), along with a discussion of your general health.

Background and expertise

With a solid foundation in midwifery, Stephanie Diaz brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to Vista Women's Health. Stephanie Diaz received her Master of Science degree in nursing and midwifery from Frontier Nursing University and her undergraduate degree from College of Saint Elizabeth. Prior to completing her graduate degree she practiced as a labor and delivery nurse at Mountainside Hospital for 6 years. Since becoming a CNM, she has worked in both hospital-based and private practices. She is an active member of the American College of Nurse-Midwives, and a member of the American Congress of Obstetricians and Gynecologists. Stephanie enjoys working with women through their transitions of life and allows women to safely and collaboratively take control of their birth outcomes. She has dedicated herself to staying up-to-date with the latest evidence-based practices and techniques in the field, continually expanding her knowledge to provide the best possible care for her patients.

Compassionate and holistic care

Stephanie Diaz has a deep sense of compassion and respect for the birthing process. She takes the time to build a strong rapport with each patient, fostering a trusting and supportive relationship. She listens attentively, values your unique desires and preferences, and works collaboratively with you on a plan that aligns with your goals.

Postpartum care and beyond

Beyond the birth itself, Stephanie is there for you during the postpartum period. She provides invaluable guidance and support as you transition into motherhood, offering breastfeeding assistance, newborn care tips, and emotional support. She is dedicated to ensuring that you have a smooth and healthy start to this new chapter, addressing any concerns and providing the resources you need to thrive.

Stephanie Diaz is honored to be a part of your birthing journey, and she is excited to guide you through this transformative experience with compassion, expertise, and unwavering support.

Our philosophy

Personalized, collaborative care.

At Vista Women's Health we believe in taking a personalized and collaborative approach to obstetrics and gynecology. We believe every woman who comes for care should be treated as an individual whether it is for an annual visit, gynecologic concern or pregnancy.

Patient-centered medical care is based on mutual trust and communication. Our providers take the time to speak with patients, get to know them, and address their needs by answering questions in terms that are easy to understand so that they can make educated decisions regarding their own health and well-being.

Professionalism and expertise are provided at every level and our patients love the individualized care they receive and the emphasis we place on the patient experience.
