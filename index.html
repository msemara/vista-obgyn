Minimally invasive surgery

Pelvic pain is not something you have to live with.

Many women wait years for an endometriosis diagnosis, or are told a large open operation is the only answer for fibroids. Dr. Shilad creates a personalized treatment plan with you and performs surgery through small incisions using laparoscopy and the da Vinci robotic system, so you recover faster and get back to your life.

The practice regularly takes on complex cases, including endometriosis or scar tissue that involves the bowel or ureters, and patients who have had surgery before.

Obstetrician-gynecologist and minimally invasive gynecologic surgeon

Common questions

Before your first visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through Zocdoc at any time, or call (973) 655-9662 during office hours. Existing patients can also message the office through the patient portal.

Where is the Montclair office?

We are in the Harries Pavilion at 1 Bay Avenue in Montclair, on the second floor in Suite 5. The phone number is the same: (973) 655-9662.

What does minimally invasive surgery mean?

The operation is done through a few small incisions with a camera and fine instruments, either laparoscopically or with the da Vinci robotic system, instead of one large incision. Most patients have less pain and recover sooner than with open surgery.

Do you treat men?

Yes, for hormone and fertility concerns. Dr. Shilad sees men for low testosterone and decreased libido, and evaluates low sperm count, often as part of a couple's fertility workup. Treatment is chosen with fertility in mind, since testosterone therapy itself lowers sperm production.

Do I have to be a patient to take a class?

No. Classes are free for Vista patients and open to other families for a fee listed above.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

Please call the office with your insurance card before your first visit so we can confirm your coverage and any out-of-pocket costs in advance.
