Pelvic pain care

Pelvic pain treatment in Montclair, NJ

Pelvic pain affects many women at different stages of life and can range from mild discomfort to chronic, life-disrupting symptoms. At Vista Women's Health, we work with you to identify the root cause of your pain and build a care plan that fits your individual needs.

Whether it's linked to your reproductive system, bladder, muscles, or another cause, our goal is to help you find long-term relief and regain comfort in your daily routine.

Causes

Common causes of pelvic pain.

Pelvic pain can be caused by several conditions. Some of the most common include:

  • Endometriosis
  • Ovarian cysts
  • Uterine fibroids
  • Pelvic inflammatory disease (PID)
  • Interstitial cystitis
  • Musculoskeletal issues (e.g., pelvic floor tension)
  • Scar tissue from prior surgeries or infections

When to get care

When to see a specialist.

Reach out to a provider if you experience:

  • Ongoing pelvic discomfort or pressure
  • Pain during menstruation, intimacy, or bowel movements
  • Pain that interferes with walking, sitting, or sleep
  • No relief from pain medications or at-home treatments

About pelvic pain

Understand pelvic pain.

  • How it feelsPelvic pain occurs mostly in the lower abdomen area. The pain might be steady, or it might come and go. It can be a sharp and stabbing pain in a specific spot or a dull pain that is spread out. If the pain is severe, it might get in the way of your daily activities.
  • Finding the causeYou might feel pain during your period or it could also happen when you have intercourse. Pelvic pain can be a sign that there is a problem with one of the organs in your pelvic area, such as the uterus, ovaries, fallopian tubes, cervix, or vagina. Some women have more than one cause of pelvic pain at the same time. You might need to have a lab, imaging, or other medical tests to find the cause of the pain. The treatment will depend on the cause, how bad the pain is, and how often it occurs.
